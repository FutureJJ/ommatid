What is real here: the wiring (which neuron connects to which) is measured from one fruit fly by electron microscopy; the optic lobe is a connectome-constrained model trained on an optic-flow task (Lappalainen et al. 2024); the spiking is a simulation with the parameters of Shiu et al. 2024. A real fly sees ~300° and mostly motion, so this is a demonstration of the pipeline, not a claim about fly perception. The live fly is at ommatid.org.
